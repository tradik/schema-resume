## The short version

The validator, converter and CV editor on this site process your document
**in your browser**. The file you paste or open is never uploaded, stored or
logged by us. You can confirm this by opening the network tab of your browser
while you use them.

## What we collect

**Server logs.** The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare records the
requests it serves (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for
security and operational purposes under its own
[privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). We do not receive
per-visitor logs from it.

**Analytics — only with your consent.** If you accept the *analytics* category
in the cookie banner, Google Analytics 4 records page views and basic
interaction events. Nothing is sent to Google before you consent, and you can
withdraw consent at any time from the [cookie policy](/cookies) page. IP
anonymisation is enabled.

**Proof of consent.** When you make a choice in the cookie banner, a record of
that choice (the categories granted, the policy version, a timestamp and a
salted hash of your IP address) is stored so we can demonstrate consent as the
GDPR requires. The hash cannot be reversed to your IP address. Records are kept
for the lifetime of the consent (180 days) and then deleted.

## What we do not collect

- The content of any resume you validate, convert or edit.
- Account or contact details — the site has no accounts and no forms.
- Cross-site tracking or advertising identifiers.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you can ask what personal data we hold about you,
ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and withdraw consent. Because the site
holds no personal data beyond the consent record described above, the practical
answer to most requests is that there is nothing to return. To ask anyway, or
to raise a concern, email <info@schema-resume.org>.
